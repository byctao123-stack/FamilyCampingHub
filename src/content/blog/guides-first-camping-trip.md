## Before You Go: Planning Phase

### Choosing Your Campsite

The right campsite makes all the difference. Here's what to look for:

**Essential Amenities:**
- Flush toilets or clean vault toilets
- Potable water access
- Level campsite with shade
- Nearby hiking trails for kids
- Camp host on-site

**Booking Platforms:**
- [Recreation.gov](https://www.recreation.gov) — National Parks & Forests
- ReserveAmerica — State parks
- KOA.com — Private campgrounds
- Hipcamp — Unique private land

**Pro tip:** Book 2-3 months ahead for summer weekends at popular locations.

## Setting Up Camp

### Arrival Checklist
1. **Arrive before dark** — Set up in daylight for the first time
2. **Choose your spot carefully** — High, flat, and sheltered
3. **Set up the tent first** — Your home base before anything else
4. **Organize your kitchen** — Cooking area downwind from sleeping area
5. **Create a fire pit area** — If fires are allowed

### Tent Setup Tips
- Clear the ground of rocks and sticks
- Use a footprint/ground tarp under the tent
- Orient the door away from prevailing wind
- Stake all guy lines even if it seems calm
- Keep the rainfly on at night (condensation happens!)

## Camping with Kids: Survival Tips

### Before the Trip
- Watch camping YouTube videos together
- Practice tent setup in the backyard
- Do a "practice campout" on the living room floor
- Talk about what to expect (bugs, bathrooms, no screens)

### At Camp
- Establish clear boundaries
- Use the buddy system for bathroom trips
- Teach kids the "three blows" whistle signal
- Keep a consistent bedtime routine
- Let kids help with camp chores

### Food & Meals
- Prep as much as possible at home
- Keep snacks accessible at all times
- Don't experiment with new recipes on the first trip
- Have backup meals ready (everyone gets hangry)

## Safety First

### Wildlife Safety
- Store ALL food in the car or bear box
- Never leave food in the tent
- Keep a safe distance from all animals
- Make noise on trails to avoid surprising animals

### Fire Safety
- Only use designated fire rings
- Keep water or dirt nearby
- Never leave fire unattended
- Fully extinguish before bed (drown, stir, drown, feel)

### Weather Preparedness
- Check the forecast daily
- Have a "bail out" plan if weather turns bad
- Know where to seek shelter
- Pack layers for temperature swings

## Leave No Trace Principles

1. Plan ahead and prepare
2. Travel and camp on durable surfaces
3. Dispose of waste properly
4. Leave what you find
5. Minimize campfire impact
6. Respect wildlife
7. Be considerate of other visitors

## Common First-Trip Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Car camping with too much stuff** — You don't need your full kitchen
2. **Not testing gear beforehand** — Always do a backyard trial
3. **Choosing a campsite too far from home** — Keep it under 2 hours
4. **Over-scheduling activities** — Leave time for relaxation
5. **Forgetting rain gear** — Even in summer, afternoon storms happen

## Final Thoughts

Your first family camping trip doesn't need to be perfect. It's about creating memories, not achieving camping perfection. The kids will remember the adventure, not the fact that the tent was slightly crooked or dinner was slightly burnt.

**Remember:** Start simple, stay flexible, and have fun! 🏕️
