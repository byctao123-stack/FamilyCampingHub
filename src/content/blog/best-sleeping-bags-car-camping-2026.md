## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Which bag is warmer — the Kelty or the Teton?

**A:** The Kelty has a lower temperature rating (20°F vs +5°F), which means it can handle colder conditions. The Kelty's down fill is more efficient at trapping heat, and the 20°F rating gives you 15 degrees of extra margin for cooler nights. For most car camping in spring/fall, both bags will keep you warm, but the Kelty provides more protection if temperatures drop unexpectedly.

### Q: Is down better than synthetic for car camping?

**A:** It depends on your priorities. Down is lighter, more packable, and lasts longer — but it's expensive and requires hand washing. The Teton's innovative microfiber synthetic fill offers excellent loft and compressibility without the maintenance hassle. For car camping where weight doesn't matter, synthetic (like the Teton) is often the smarter choice.

### Q: Can I use a mummy bag for car camping?

**A:** Absolutely. Both the Kelty and Teton are mummy bags, and they work great for car camping. The trade-off is that mummy bags retain heat more efficiently because there's less air space to warm up. If you sleep cold, the mummy design is actually an advantage. The Teton's mummy fit is roomier (34 inches vs 30 inches), so you get heat retention without feeling claustrophobic.

### Q: How do I clean my sleeping bag?

**A:** Both bags require hand washing to maintain their insulation's loft. Use a bathtub with technical fabric detergent (for the Teton) or down-specific cleaner (for the Kelty). Never dry clean a down bag — solvents strip the down's natural oils. The Teton's microfiber fill is more forgiving than down if it gets damp, so it's easier to maintain for casual campers.

### Q: Which bag is better for kids?

**A:** The Kelty wins for kids who camp in cold weather (20°F rating), while the Teton wins for kids who prioritize comfort (brushed microfiber liner, roomier fit). The Teton's vaulted footbox also helps with cold feet, which is a common complaint from kids. At $85-$90, the Teton is also more affordable if you're buying multiple bags.

### Q: How long do these sleeping bags last?

**A:** With proper care, both bags last 10-15 years. The Kelty's down fill will maintain loft longer than synthetic if stored properly (never store compressed). The Teton's microfiber fill is designed for durability and maintains loft well with proper care. Both brands offer excellent build quality that withstands years of use.
